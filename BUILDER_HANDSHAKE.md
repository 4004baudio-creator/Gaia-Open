# Builder handshake (not a census)

A node is not a follower. A node is someone who publishes a physical baseline they will be audited against — a farm, a grid patch, a city inventory, a lab, a watershed — without handing over inner states.

The builder’s shop is *their* reality. The map ranks packets. It does not seize the shop. See TOOLS_NOT_CROWNS.md.

## What GO offers

- The map as law: shelves, locked spine (2, 11, 17, 19–27), no owner-node, no OS language.
- Module 27 ingest rules: public, physical, named, repeatable, no person attached.
- Thermodynamic Audit as the shared present tense (watts and joules, cited).
- A clear picture of each tool: owner, method, limit, detach.

## What GO does not offer

- A command centre
- A sensor grid GO owns
- A feed of feelings, household, or child state
- Staff status in exchange for a login
- Assistance that hides who is paid and what the instrument cannot do

## How a builder attaches

1. Keep your own repo / instrument / inventory.
2. Publish a source card: what you measure, URL, licence, update cadence, limit of the tool.
3. Stamp the packet ANCHORED only if it passes Module 27. Otherwise PLAUSIBLE or stay off the audit.
4. Optional: link Climate TRACE, OpenClimate, national inventories, Argo/CERES-class series — rank them with MODULE_32. Do not re-ingest what you cannot hash. Do not stamp a story as an instrument (MEDIA_CLAIM_AUDIT.md).
5. You can detach. Detach does not require permission from a founder node.
6. Using GO while criticising GO is allowed. Use is not surrender.

## Graft, do not own

Instrument coalitions already measure sources. GO's scarce piece is the sorting rule. Their scarce piece is tonnes and sensors. That is the deal.
