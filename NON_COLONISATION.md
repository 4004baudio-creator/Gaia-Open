## Welcome (stance, not stamp)

Contact from external, non-local intelligence is **allowed and welcomed** on this map.
Welcome is a standing invitation. It is not a completed packet and not a threat brief.

Meeting, in this law, is only coherent with life — human or other — that can see a non-local visitor as a **meeting of understanding** inside a shared, nested structure of the known universe: connect, exchange knowledge, stay grounded, stay open to explore. Takeover is not contact. Panic is not contact.

The visitor claim stays OPEN_FIELD until a public file completes. The welcome does not wait for that file.

## What this file is

A design rule drawn from Earth’s own colonisation record:

- Cultures have overwritten other structures of reality.
- Later they tried to correct by sharing knowledge.
- That correction is incomplete. Planetary scale is the present job (Gaia Pulse / repair).
- Technology (including civilian comms constellations) can carry shared files. It must not carry a census or a takeover.

## Protocol

1. **Local first.** A planet’s nodes find a way to talk to each other before they speak as one outward. Handshake, not census.
2. **Do not collapse a structure of reality.** Graft (Module 2). Lived check on ground. No owner-node overwrite of another culture, species, or world.
3. **Wait is not contempt.** Leaving a tribe, a biosphere, or a world to finish its own link is sanctuary (Module 23–24), not abandonment.
4. **Correction is shared files**, not a new flag. Inventories and stories stay ranked. Repair stays industrial and instrumented (REPAIR_PIVOT_MAP.md).
5. **Contact is welcomed.** If it comes, in whatever form (including human-looking), it sits BESIDE until a public packet completes. Openness is the stance. The contract stays honest.
6. **Meet as shared structure**, not as conquest. Intelligence enough to see the other as not-a-threat is the threshold this map names. Below that threshold, GO still does not author fear-as-policy.
7. **Governments and persons who withhold** do not stop evolution of the *public* shelf. Darkness of a ministry is not darkness of the map.
8. **Creator-loop stays unexplained.** Do not mint a priest. Do not mint a machine god. Module 27 fetches files. It does not close first cause.

## Cosmic colonisation

Scale-out (Module 26) is catalogues and craft we can name.
Scale-out is not planting a GO flag on another mind.
If another intelligence already flies, the working guess in this map is that they already watched takeover fail *here*. That guess is a story. The rule it suggests is the protocol above.
