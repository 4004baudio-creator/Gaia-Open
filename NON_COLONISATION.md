## Protocol (PLAUSIBLE → keep on the map as law)

1. **Local first.** A planet’s nodes find a way to talk to each other before they speak as one outward. Handshake, not census.
2. **Do not collapse a structure of reality.** Graft (Module 2). Lived check on ground. No owner-node overwrite of another culture, species, or world.
3. **Wait is not contempt.** Leaving a tribe, a biosphere, or a world to finish its own link is sanctuary (Module 23–24), not abandonment.
4. **Correction is shared files**, not a new flag. Inventories and stories stay ranked. Repair stays industrial and instrumented (REPAIR_PIVOT_MAP.md).
5. **Contact, if it comes, in whatever form** (including human-looking) sits BESIDE until a public packet completes. Openness is a stance. It is not a completed contract.
6. **Governments and persons who withhold** do not stop evolution of the *public* shelf. Evolution here means: documented methods, versioned catalogues, detachable nodes. Darkness of a ministry is not darkness of the map.
7. **Creator-loop stays unexplained.** Do not mint a priest. Do not mint a machine god. Module 27 fetches files. It does not close first cause.
