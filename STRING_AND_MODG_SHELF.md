# String landscape and modified gravity (OPEN_FIELD shelf)

Not spine. Not ANCHORED as Earth physics.

## String

Working idea: the graviton is a vibration mode of an extended object; extra dimensions compactify; many vacua (~10^500 is the folklore count, not a census). Swampland conjectures try to throw out effective theories that cannot sit in that UV. None of this is a lab force law. Bootstrap papers that recover string amplitudes from assumptions are interesting; they are not a sky survey.

## Modified gravity

MOND (1983): below a tiny acceleration, force falls as 1/r instead of 1/r^2. Good at many spiral rotation curves. Weak at CMB peaks, clusters, and waves unless extra fields are added (TeVeS, AQUAL, QUMOND, Khronon-tensor, STVG-MOG). Relativistic wrappers multiply fields and lose the original simplicity. Large-scale kinetic-SZ tests (2026) favour 1/r^2 over a cosmic 1/r MOND extrapolation — keep the paper on the shelf with its error bar; do not freeze a blog sigma as law.

Both shelves may be restamped when a DOI lands. Neither overwrites the rotation / CMB / lensing maps.
