# Biosample packet (supporting)

Not spine. Sits beside PACKET_CONTRACT.md, EDGE_DEVICE_AUDIT.md, TOOLS_NOT_CROWNS.md.

## Split

A **public model atlas** (AlphaFold DB, AlphaGenome Atlas, DeepVariant weights) is a prediction file. Licence + version + “not a measured body.”

A **blood unit or tissue** is a contract. Default purpose is the named clinical use (transfusion). Research is a second, dated consent. No silent genotype-while-we-are-here.

## Rank

Peer-reviewed structure or sequenced public reference > model atlas lookup > company blog > vacuum-phantom story.

Household genomes, donation deferrals, and “I offered my blood” stay off ingest.

## Google stack as channel, not crown

DeepVariant / AlphaFold / AlphaGenome / Antigravity Science Skills may be watched as public tools. They do not own DNA. They do not replace a human sign-off on a living sample.
