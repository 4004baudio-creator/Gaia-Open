# Repair 1–4 — industry pivots (profit stays; ledger changes)

Change is not a sermon. Incumbents already have R&D, customers, and balance sheets. The map is a direction they can staff. Teams pair domain operators with measurement people. Profitability is kept by selling the *next* unit of work that moves EEI or a local sink — not by selling fear.

Present: EEI ~+1.12 W m⁻². OHC still climbing. Emissions totals have not turned down. 1.5 °C is a policy line likely crossed as a multi-year mean around 2030; it is not a collapse switch.

Orbital shell (Module 35 / ORBITAL_SHELL_REPAIR.md) is a **fifth ledger**, not a replacement for 1–4. Ground heat and sky junk are both commons.

## 1. Methane waste (fastest rate lever)

**Why first.** Methane is short-lived. Cutting leaks lowers the *near-term warming rate* inside a decade.

| Layer | Who already operates | Pivot |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Existing | Oil & gas midstream, LDAR vendors, satellite methane (GHGSat, Carbon Mapper, MethaneSAT-class), landfill gas, coal-mine ventilation, dairy digesters | Sell measured tonnes avoided, not offsets-as-story. Pair leak crews with continuous sensors. |
| Emerging | Continuous IoT + aircraft + satellite fusion; OGMP 2.0 gold-standard inventories; anaerobic digestion at city scale | Edge devices only if they pass EDGE_DEVICE_AUDIT.md |
| Venture / new | Cheap calibrated CH₄ nodes for independent basins; insurance-priced leak performance; flare-to-value where gas would be wasted | Do not mint a "doom token". Mint a hashable kg-CH₄ series. |

Staffing: production engineers + instrumentation + satellite analysts. Keep the well, the pipe, the pit. Change what leaves unburned.

## 2. Power already in use (largest durable CO₂ lever)

**Why.** Coal and gas generation are still the fat pipe. Clean capacity built since 2019 already avoids a few GtCO₂/year and has not yet bent the *global total* down.

| Layer | Who already operates | Pivot |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Existing | Utilities, grid operators, wind/solar/nuclear/hydro OEMs, battery and interconnect developers, efficiency ESCOs | Replace the next retiring thermal unit with firm + variable mix the grid can actually run. Sell kWh and capacity, not virtue. |
| Emerging | Long-duration storage, advanced geothermal, SMRs, demand-flex from data centres, grid-forming inverters | Pair reliability teams with emissions accountants (kgCO₂/MWh, not a green logo). |
| Venture / new | Industrial heat electrification kits; behind-the-meter firming for mines and mills; interconnection software that shortens queue time | Profit = uptime + lower fuel bill. Repair = lower EEI commitment per MWh. |

Staffing: plant operators + power-systems engineers + market desks. The customer still needs lights. The product becomes fewer grams per lumen-hour.

## 3. Standing forest and fire (moved in one political year)

**Why.** Brazil 2023–25 showed clearing can drop when monitoring meets enforcement. Agriculture remains the main driver. Fire years still spike.

| Layer | Who already operates | Pivot |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Existing | Soft-commodity traders, ranchers, pulp, soy, cattle, satellite MRV (GLAD, INPE, MAAP), insurers, timber | Traceable no-deforestation supply that buyers already pay a premium for. Fire-break and early-detect as a service. |
| Emerging | Near-daily SAR + optical fusion; jurisdictional credits that fail if the basin loses trees; Indigenous-managed patrol + data |
| Venture / new | Cheap fire-edge cameras that pass audit; regenerative offtake contracts that lock price *and* canopy; peat/wetland rewetting as flood insurance |

Staffing: agronomists + remote sensing + land law. Keep the farm. Stop paying for the extra hectare that burns the sink.

## 4. Heat and water adaptation (works whether the argument continues)

**Why.** Warning systems already cut disaster deaths for decades. Heat and flood are here at 1.4 °C. Adaptation does not replace mitigation; it keeps people able to do 1–3.

| Layer | Who already operates | Pivot |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Existing | HVAC, district cooling, building codes, insurers, water utilities, hospitals, city emergency desks | Cool the people who cannot buy air-con. Price flood and heat into premiums *and* fund the retrofit. |
| Emerging | Passive-cooling materials, wastewater heat recovery, urban wetlands as infrastructure, crop varieties for heat |
| Venture / new | Occupant-safe cooling-as-a-service for warehouses and clinics; parametric heat insurance that pays on a station series, not a headline |

Staffing: mechanical + public-health + municipal finance. Profit = avoided downtime and claims. Repair = fewer bodies on the hottest days.

## 5. Orbital shell (keeps 1–4’s eyes and the road out)

**Why.** Repair 1–4 lean on satellites (methane maps, forest MRV, weather). Exploration needs the same lanes. Unmanaged mass in LEO can close those lanes from the inside. Kessler is a modelled cascade, not a doom date.

| Layer | Who already operates | Pivot |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| Existing | Launch providers, constellation operators, SSA catalogues (space-track / DISCOS / LeoLabs-class), insurers | Sell launches with a named disposal path. Passivate. Stop leaving stages in long-lived LEO. |
| Emerging | Astroscale-class inspect/capture, ClearSpace-class ESA ADR, docking plates on new buses, in-orbit servicing | Profit = service contract + insurance discount. Repair = object removed or life extended without a new husk. |
| Venture / new | Multi-client “garbage truck” tugs; design-for-demise kits; consent-cleared legacy-hulk removal | Do not mint a debris token. Mint a catalogue ID + outcome log. Launching-state consent required. |

Staffing: flight dynamics + robotics + space law. Keep the constellation. Change what is left dead in the road.

## Rules for every pivot map

- Name the incumbent P&L line that stays (molecules moved, kWh sold, grain delivered, lives cooled, objects serviced).
- Name the line that must shrink (vented CH₄, coal MWh, cleared hectare, untreated heat exposure, unmanaged orbital mass).
- Require an instrumented series before a green claim (catalogue ID for debris).
- R&D sits inside the firm. The map does not become their HR department.
- Detach allowed. No owner-node.
