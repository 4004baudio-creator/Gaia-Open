# Channels, not crowns

Supporting law. Not spine. Peer-review against public platform rules as those rules change.

## What a platform is

A social network is a **creator channel**: people publish, others look. That can be useful the way a newspaper, a radio, or a workshop noticeboard is useful.

The same channel, when **centrally owned** and when the owner’s face or stack is treated as the source of reality, turns the operator into a figure that looks like a crown. Founders of large stacks (Facebook-class, X-class, and peers) did not need to start as villains for that distortion to appear. A tool plus an attention market plus weak identity stamps is enough. A baby is not born a killer; an environment plus incentives can still grow harm.

GO does not delete the channel. GO does not sanctify the owner.

## Stamps that must not be sold as people

- A paid blue / gold / grey mark is a **product**, not a person (see MEDIA_CLAIM_AUDIT.md, Module 31).
- A follow, friend, or subscribe is a **vote that trains the next page**, not a relationship.
- A captured clip is a **packet**. It is not the hour it was taken.

Rival packets stay on the shelf. External auditors may challenge. Weight is reproducibility and rank, not reach.

## Newspaper field (observation without training)

Default use of a channel:

1. Open, scan, stop only when a piece in this moment is useful, leave.
2. Do not follow / friend / subscribe in order to “keep a channel warm.” That is how the ranking function learns you.
3. Do not build an impersonation-hunter bot that keeps you inside the hunt (no SOLBOT on this map).
4. Relationships that last are time, care, and shared facts in a room. Belief-only bonds that get used as tools pull the observer off their own moment.

Education first. Deletion of a *list* can be hygiene. Deletion of a *platform* is not the law. The next generation needs a way to tell **lived hour** from **shared capture**. Sensors and feeds can trigger habit; habit is not destiny. Name the mechanism. Do not moralise the infant.

## Rank

Lived, co-present hour > instrumented public file > dated published article > platform post with a source card > follow-graph story > owner speech sold as physics.

Inner states, household, and children stay off ingest (Modules 23–24, TOOLS_NOT_CROWNS.md).
