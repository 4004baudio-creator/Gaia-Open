# Instruments as tools, not crowns

Supporting law. Not spine. Serves Modules 11, 23, 25 and 2.
Packet gate: LANE_FILTER.md + PACKET_CONTRACT.md.
Contact: NON_COLONISATION.md. Handshake: BUILDER_HANDSHAKE.md.

Earth is a nested shared field. Each node keeps an individual reality inside it.
Instruments (satellites, grids, catalogues, this map, a licence, a feed) can *assist* those realities. They become control when they shape another node’s world without a clear picture of what the tool is.

## Portrayal

If the overall image of a tool is not clear — who built it, what it measures, who is paid, what it cannot do — assistance reads as control. That is the bite of the hand that feeds: people use a creation, complain of it, then build with it anyway. The conflict is not solved by banning the tool. It is solved by **honest stamp**.

| Portrayal | Lane |
|---|---|
| Named instrument + method + limit | Tool — may assist |
| “We help you” with no file, no detach, no rival | Illusion of assistance → treat as control attempt |
| Builder’s own P&L and craft | Their reality — allowed. Rank their packet, do not seize the shop |
| Immediate household / child / inner state | Sanctuary (11, 23). Never collective ingest |

## Protocol

1. **Nested, not flattened.** A shared planet does not cancel individual realities. Rule of 25 stands.
2. **Tool, not crown.** An instrument that writes another node’s story without a source card is refused the ANCHORED stamp.
3. **Builders have a reality too.** The firm that flies the satellite or runs the grid is not a villain by existing. Their creation may be used and criticised in the same day. GO ranks the *packet*, not the person.
4. **Use is not surrender.** Taking a pipe (Starlink, a catalogue, a licence) does not hand the node to the pipe’s owner. Detach remains.
5. **Clear image required.** Missing owner, method, limit, or rival → BESIDE. Incomplete portrayal cannot complete.
6. **Immediate state is local.** Restrictions on a node’s own body, household, or child are corrected *there* — graft on the ground (Module 2). They are not a feed for the map and not a reason to crown a tool.
7. **Complaint + use is allowed.** Hypocrisy of “I hate it / I use it” is human, not a crime on this map. What is refused is selling that conflict as a single official reality.

## Weekly check

Flag chrome that: treats GO as a command over nodes; harvests household or child state; sells assistance without a source card; flattens builders into enemies or subjects.
