# EEI and OHC in plain language

Shelf: anchored terms only. Cycles exist. A cycle is not a licence to ignore a measured imbalance.

## Earth's Energy Imbalance (EEI)

Imagine Earth as a room with a heater and an open window.

- Energy *in*: sunlight the planet absorbs.
- Energy *out*: heat and light the planet radiates to space.
- EEI is the difference.

If in and out match, the room stays at a steady heat content. That is thermodynamic equilibrium.

Present tense (IGCC / CERES-class, 2013–2025): EEI is about **+1.12 watts per square metre**. The heater is running a little harder than the window can dump. The planet is still accumulating heat. Mandate on this map: drive EEI toward **0**. Do not stamp equilibrium while the number is positive.

One watt per square metre, over the whole Earth, is hundreds of terawatts of continuous extra heat. Most of it does not stay in the air. It goes into water.

## Ocean Heat Content (OHC)

The ocean is the battery.

About nine-tenths of the extra energy ends up in seawater. OHC is the running total of that stored heat, usually quoted for the top 2000 metres (the layer Argo floats actually profile) and sometimes for the full depth.

Units: zettajoules (1 ZJ = 10^21 joules). 2025 was another record year for 0–200 m / 0–2000 m inventories across independent products (IAP, NOAA/NCEI, Copernicus). Surface temperature can dip in a La Niña year while OHC still rises. Temperature is the weather of the battery. OHC is the charge.

## How to use them without narrative

| Question | Look at |
|----------|---------|
| Is the planet still charging? | EEI sign and multi-year mean |
| Where did the charge go? | ΔOHC, 0–2000 m, two products |
| Did this year "fix" it? | Not SST rank alone |
| Are we at equilibrium? | Only if EEI is indistinguishable from 0 |

Natural cycles (seasons, El Niño / La Niña, orbital bands) move weather. They do not cancel a multi-decade positive EEI. Mis-observing a cycle as the whole story is how both "nothing is happening" and "the world ends Tuesday" get built.
